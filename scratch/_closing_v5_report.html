Formula & the BOM-conversion adjustment

Closing[group, unit]  =  Opening + Inward + Inf-In + Xfr-In − Xfr-Out + CN − Outward + Adj-In − Adj-Out ± Rearr

Outward is every sale deducted from the article's own group (Trail Mix sale → Trail Mix down). Inter-company invoices (Candor Foods / Candor Dates as buyer) are excluded — they're billing-only transfers.
Adj-In cancels the FG outward (Trail Mix sale → Trail Mix back up).
Adj-Out deducts the BOM-derived ingredients from each source RM group (Trail Mix sale → almond, cashew, raisin etc. all go down per the BOM recipe).
± Rearr auto-rebalances cells that would close negative against units with stock for the same group (data-entry mistakes where the wrong dispatch warehouse was on the invoice). Sums to zero per group across units.

Net effect: FG-only groups (Trail Mix, Bars, Hampers) stay flat; the underlying RM groups absorb the real cost.
